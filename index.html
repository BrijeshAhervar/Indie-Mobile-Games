To deliver seamless offline and in-browser gameplay without forcing account sign-ins, our games store gameplay state locally on your device (utilizing Unity PlayerPrefs on mobile and LocalStorage/IndexedDB in WebGL browsers):

  • Gameplay Progression: Unlocked level milestones, 3-star stage ratings, high score records, earned virtual coins/gems, and achievement progress.
  • Audio & Interface Settings: Sound effect volume, background music toggle, screen orientation preferences, and selected language.

Player Control: This progression data remains stored solely within your local device or browser sandbox. You can reset or permanently delete all local game data at any time by clearing your browser cache/storage or uninstalling the mobile application.

  • CrazyGames B.V. (Unity Web SDK / Web API): Serves non-intrusive, family-friendly WebGL advertisements and ensures appropriate frequency capping. Review their practices at the CrazyGames Privacy Policy.
  • Unity Ads / Google AdMob (Mobile & WebGL): Delivers contextual, platform-compliant video advertisements. Review the Unity Privacy Policy and Google Privacy Policy.

These partner SDKs may automatically process non-identifying technical metadata (such as device manufacturer, screen resolution, operating system version, coarse country-level geolocation derived from IP, and ad interaction timestamps) solely for ad delivery verification, fraud prevention, and performance telemetry.

Protecting the privacy of young players is of paramount importance to us. Our games are designed for general audiences and family-friendly play, strictly complying with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), and mobile store family safety guidelines.

  • We do not knowingly solicit, collect, or process personal data from children under the age of 13 (or under 16 in certain European jurisdictions).

  • If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately, and we will take prompt steps to verify and permanently delete such records.

6. Global Player Rights (GDPR, UK GDPR & CCPA/CPRA)

Depending on your location, you hold specific legal rights regarding data protection:

  • EEA & UK Players (GDPR): You have the right to request access to, rectification of, or erasure of any technical telemetry processed by our partners, and the right to object to or restrict processing based on legitimate interest.
  • California Residents (CCPA / CPRA): You have the right to know what categories of data are collected and to request deletion. We do not sell or share personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising.

We employ industry-standard security safeguards. Web communications to our portal and third-party APIs are encrypted via Transport Layer Security (TLS/HTTPS).
