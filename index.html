Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 2026 | Developer: Brijesh Ahervar (Indie Mobile Games)

🛡️

Zero Tracking Promise: Indie Mobile Games does NOT collect, sell, or monetize personal identifiers, device contacts, microphone audio, camera feeds, or precise GPS locations. Our games are built strictly for safe, fun entertainment.

1. Scope of This Policy

This Privacy Policy applies to all mobile games and web browser applications published by Brijesh Ahervar / Indie Mobile Games, including our flagship title Wrong Tap and any future game releases.

2. Information We Do NOT Collect

We believe players deserve respect and transparency. Our games:

  • Do NOT collect your legal name, physical address, email address, or phone number.
  • Do NOT access device cameras, photo galleries, or microphones.
  • Do NOT track or log precise GPS coordinates.
  • Do NOT sell user profiling data to third-party data brokers.

3. Local Storage & Player Progression

To maintain your game progression without requiring forced accounts or logins, our games store data locally on your device (via encrypted Unity PlayerPrefs / browser LocalStorage):

  • Progress Data: Level unlocks, 3-star scores, earned coins, and milestone achievements.
  • Player Preferences: Music and sound effect volume levels, screen orientation, and selected language.

This data stays on your device and can be reset at any time by clearing your browser cache or app storage.

4. Third-Party Advertising & Platform SDKs

Our games may be distributed on platforms such as CrazyGames and mobile app stores. To support free gameplay, optional advertisements (such as Rewarded Video Ads for extra lives or victory bonuses) may be served via trusted partners:

  • CrazyGames SDK (v3 Web API): Delivers family-friendly, non-intrusive advertisements on HTML5 web builds. CrazyGames Privacy Policy
  • Unity Ads / Google AdMob: Serves contextual, child-safe ads compliant with platform standards.

These SDKs may process anonymized, non-personal diagnostic telemetry (such as device model, OS version, or approximate country) strictly to serve valid ad impressions.

5. Children's Online Privacy (COPPA & Global Compliance)

Our games are designed for general audiences and family-friendly play. We strictly adhere to the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and global child safety guidelines. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

6. Player Rights & Contacting Us

Under GDPR, CCPA, and global privacy frameworks, you have the right to request information about data practices or request assistance resetting progression.

For any privacy questions, contact the developer at: support@indiemobilegames.com (or reach out via our GitHub profile: github.com/BrijeshAhervar).
